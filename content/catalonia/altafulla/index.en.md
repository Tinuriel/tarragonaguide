⏱ 15–20 minutes from Tarragona.

One of the cosiest towns on the coast. Unlike lively Salou and La Pineda, it has a calm atmosphere: narrow streets, white houses, small squares and restaurants with sea views.

It's a lovely place to:

- stroll around the historic centre;
- relax on a calm sandy beach;
- have dinner at one of the seaside restaurants;
- climb up to the medieval Castell d'Altafulla.

Nearby stands one of the most beautiful castles on the coast — **Tamarit Castle (Castell de Tamarit)** ({{< map "ChIJq6qqqpn6oxIRkeOcc1Sg4mM" >}}), right above the sea. It's one of the most photographed spots on the Costa Daurada.
