⏱ 15 minutes from Tarragona.

The best-known resort on the Costa Daurada, with wide sandy beaches, a long palm-lined promenade and a big choice of hotels.

The main reason to come here is **PortAventura World**, one of the largest amusement parks in Europe. The complex includes:

- **PortAventura Park** ({{< map "ChIJGUCil7tZoRIRJtbCrqsasJs" >}}) — a theme park with rides for the whole family.
- **Ferrari Land** ({{< map "ChIJBw3vv5ZZoRIRLhyEYJknk00" >}}) — a Ferrari-themed park with one of the tallest and fastest roller coasters in Europe.
- **Caribe Aquatic Park** ({{< map "ChIJyw1EMpZZoRIRtiyv-J3ANlQ" >}}) — a large water park with slides and pools.
