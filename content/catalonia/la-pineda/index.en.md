⏱ 10 minutes from Tarragona.

A quiet resort with a wide sandy beach, perfect for a family holiday with children.

### Main spots

- **Aquopolis Costa Dorada** ({{< map "ChIJwT7Pk_hYoRIRoseIlrzHsQc" >}}) — one of the largest water parks in the region.
- **Aquum Spa & Wellness** ({{< map "ChIJvW_z0f5YoRIRRbAVase-97o" >}}) — a large modern spa complex with saunas, steam baths, pools and relaxation areas.
