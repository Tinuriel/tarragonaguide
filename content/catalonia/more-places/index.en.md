- **Les Ferreres Roman Aqueduct (Pont del Diable)** ({{< map "ChIJZypLVgT9oxIRgW6d0oSjB6k" >}}) — just 4 km from central Tarragona. It's about a 10–15 minute drive, and if you fancy it you can even walk there in around 45–50 minutes. A great short outing or a half-day walk.
- **Siurana** ({{< map "ChIJ595IjMJKoRIRo18yciJOMbQ" >}}) — this one is a proper day out. The mountain village is about 50 km from Tarragona, roughly an hour's drive. People come for the breathtaking views, medieval lanes, hiking trails and cliffs that are considered one of the best climbing spots in Europe.
