## ☕ Olo Café ⭐ My favourite

{{< place "ChIJwzVVCAD9oxIRY0FuHxeWPys" >}}

The newest and, in my view, the best specialty coffee shop in Tarragona. It's right in the heart of the [Old Town]({{< relref "/sights/part-alta" >}}). They use their own house-roasted beans, roasted nearby in El Vendrell.

Be sure to try:

- classic espresso and filter coffee;
- a large selection of cold coffee drinks;
- matcha;
- tasty sandwiches and pastries.

There's also a very pleasant terrace in the Old Town — a perfect spot for breakfast or a short break during a walk.

## ☕ Casa Quadrat

{{< place "ChIJ6_kEYtb9oxIRaQ4xoJHXQG8" >}}

The first specialty coffee shop in the Old Town. It played a big role in getting specialty coffee culture going in Tarragona's historic centre.

I especially recommend the milk-based drinks — they use farm-sourced Catalan milk supplied specifically for coffee shops, which brings out the coffee's flavour beautifully.

Also worth trying: signature sandwiches; breakfasts and brunches; in the evening, natural Spanish wines from small producers.

## ☕ Odila Specialty Coffee

{{< place "ChIJjcrexO79oxIRtL3D1K3I-hk" >}}

A small, cosy coffee shop with excellent specialty coffee and a very friendly atmosphere. A great spot for a quality coffee as you pass through the city centre.

## ☕ Nicoffee

{{< place "ChIJn7sMKGD9oxIRvfkZupvR9mk" >}}

The newest specialty coffee shop in the city, opened in summer 2026.

I especially recommend the matcha and the signature coffee drinks. The barista trained and worked alongside a Ukrainian barista. To me that's a good sign of quality, since Ukrainian coffee culture is considered one of the strongest in Europe today.
