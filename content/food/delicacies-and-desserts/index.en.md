## 🫒 Olive oil

Spain is the world's largest producer of olive oil. In Catalonia you'll often be served local first cold-pressed oil (Extra Virgen), which is a gastronomic highlight in its own right.

## 🧀 Spanish cheeses

Spain produces more than 150 kinds of cheese. The best known:

- **Manchego** — Spain's most famous sheep's-milk cheese. It comes young, aged and very aged.
- **Idiazábal** — a smoked sheep's-milk cheese from the Basque Country.
- **Mahón** — a cheese from the island of Menorca with a light saltiness.
- **Garrotxa** — a delicate Catalan goat's-milk cheese.
- **Tupí** — an unusual aged Catalan cheese, sometimes made with a dash of liqueur.

## 🍖 Sobrasada

A soft, spreadable sausage made with paprika. It comes from Mallorca but is easy to find all over Spain. Delicious on fresh bread or toast.

## 🍑 Membrillo

Spanish quince paste. Despite the name, its texture is closer to a firm jelly. It's traditionally served with Manchego cheese — a classic Spanish pairing.

## 🌰 Marcona

If you see Marcona almonds for sale, be sure to try them. They're called the "king of almonds" for their sweet flavour and tender texture.

## 🍮 Desserts

**Crema Catalana ⭐** — Catalonia's most famous dessert. It's similar to crème brûlée but made with lemon and orange zest and cinnamon. On top is a thin caramel crust that you crack with a spoon.

**Mel i Mató** — a very simple Catalan dessert: fresh soft mató cheese with honey. Light, fresh and not too sweet.

**Churros con Chocolate** — although this dish comes from central Spain, it's loved all over the country. Crispy sticks of choux pastry served with thick hot chocolate.

**Carquinyolis** — a traditional Catalan almond biscuit, similar to Italian biscotti. Wonderful with coffee.

**Turrón** — if you're visiting at Christmas, be sure to try turrón, the famous Spanish almond nougat. There are many kinds: soft, hard, chocolate, with pistachios and other nuts.
