**Tarragona** is one of the oldest cities in Spain and the former capital of the Roman province of Hispania Citerior. More than 2,000 years ago, the city — then called *Tarraco* — was one of the most important centres of the Roman Empire on the Iberian Peninsula. Today the historic centre and the main Roman monuments are on the UNESCO World Heritage list.

Unlike the busy resorts of Salou and La Pineda, Tarragona is a calm city where you can combine time by the sea with strolls through old streets, ancient monuments, cosy restaurants and beautiful viewpoints.
