Not to be confused with [Platja Llarga in Tarragona]({{< relref "/beaches/platja-llarga" >}}). This [Salou]({{< relref "/catalonia/salou" >}}) beach is in the Cap Salou area and is considered one of the most beautiful beaches in the resort. It's surrounded by pine forest, away from the busiest tourist areas, and is usually less crowded than Salou's central beaches. The sea is calm and the natural setting is lovely.
