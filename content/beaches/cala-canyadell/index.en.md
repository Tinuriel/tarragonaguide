A small, picturesque cove between [Altafulla]({{< relref "/catalonia/altafulla" >}}) and Torredembarra. This is one of the cosiest and most beautiful beaches on the coast: golden sand, clear water, and pine trees and rocks framing the cove. Because it's so small, it's usually much quieter here than on the larger beaches. You can reach it on foot along the coastal path from Altafulla or Torredembarra.
