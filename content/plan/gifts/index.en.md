If you'd like to take home something more than an ordinary fridge magnet — a little piece of Catalonia — look to the local gourmet products.

## 🫒 Olive oil

Tarragona is close to the Siurana DOP area, one of the best-known olive-oil-producing regions in Spain. The oil from here has a rich, fruity flavour with a slight peppery kick and is considered among the best in the country.

{{< tip >}}💡 If you're visiting in autumn, be sure to buy new-harvest oil (aceite nuevo). It's especially aromatic and considered a real delicacy.{{< /tip >}}

## 🥩 Jamón Ibérico

One of the great gastronomic symbols of Spain. For a gift, it's easiest to buy jamón already sliced and vacuum-packed — that way it travels well.

## 🍾 Cava

Catalonia is the home of cava, Spain's famous sparkling wine. The Penedès region, not far from Tarragona, produces most of the country's best cavas.

Cava makes a great gift and is often noticeably cheaper than comparable sparkling wines in other countries.

## 🥜 Nuts

Spain is famous for its nuts, especially almonds. Look out for Marcona almonds, considered among the best in the world for their sweet flavour and tender texture.

## 🍑 Jams and marmalades

Catalonia makes excellent jams from local fruit. I especially recommend:

- peach;
- apricot;
- orange.

Also worth trying is traditional membrillo — a dense quince paste usually served with cheese.

## 🧀 Spanish cheeses

If you can carry food in a cool bag, buy an aged Manchego or one of the local Catalan cheeses. They go beautifully with quince paste and a glass of cava.

## 🍫 Turrón

One of the best-known Spanish sweet souvenirs. Classic almond turrón is sold all year round, and at **Torrons Vicens** ({{< map "ChIJh6QO7dP8oxIR7nuqJJBs2hE" >}}) you'll find both traditional and modern signature flavours.

{{< tip >}}💡 My perfect set of edible souvenirs from Tarragona: a bottle of Siurana olive oil, a bottle of cava, Jamón Ibérico, Marcona almonds, turrón and a jar of good orange or apricot jam. It captures the taste of Catalonia perfectly and makes a wonderful gift for your loved ones.{{< /tip >}}

## 🐟 Tinned fish

Spain is known not only for fresh seafood but also for some of the best tinned fish in the world. It's a great souvenir: it keeps for a long time, travels easily and is of high quality.

Look out for:

- 🐟 anchovies from Cantabria (Anchoas del Cantábrico) — considered the best in Spain for their rich flavour and tender texture;
- 🐠 sardines in olive oil;
- 🦑 squid and cuttlefish in their own sauce or ink;
- 🐙 octopus;
- 🦪 mussels in escabeche (Mejillones en escabeche) — one of the most popular Spanish tinned products;
- 🐚 cockles (Berberechos) — small shellfish considered a real delicacy.

{{< tip >}}💡 The best selection of tinned fish can usually be found in the gourmet food hall at **El Corte Inglés** ({{< map "ChIJUUjnli_9oxIRyPbPuBhI38E" >}}). Spanish tinned fish isn't "camping food" — it's a genuine gourmet product that many restaurants serve as a starter in its own right, with a glass of wine or cava.{{< /tip >}}
