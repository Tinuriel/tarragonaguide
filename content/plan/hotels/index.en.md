There aren't very many hotels in Tarragona itself, since the city isn't a classic beach resort. Many tourists coming for a seaside holiday choose the neighbouring towns of Salou and La Pineda instead. Tarragona tends to attract visitors who come for the history, for walks through the old town, or on cruise ships.

## Recommended hotels

- **H10 Imperial Tarraco** ({{< map "ChIJWbnnx0b9oxIR9DVVlk8OgMA" >}}) — arguably the best hotel in the city. It's right across from the Roman amphitheatre, with stunning views of both the Mediterranean and the amphitheatre. There's a rooftop terrace with a pool and a panoramic view of the historic centre.
- **SB Ciutat de Tarragona** ({{< map "ChIJy066pS79oxIR7gDz23Ubq_I" >}}) — a good city hotel in the modern centre of Tarragona. Convenient for walking around the city, close to the train station, the bus station and the shopping streets.
- **Hotel Astari** ({{< map "ChIJMYP5CMz8oxIRg940NNUL_6k" >}}) — in a quiet area, about a 10–15 minute walk from Arrabassada beach and the historic centre. A good option if you want to combine beach time with walks around the old town.
