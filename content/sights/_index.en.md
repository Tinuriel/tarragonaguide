Tarragona is the ancient Tarraco: a Roman amphitheatre right by the sea, a circus with underground galleries, city walls and a charming old town, plus viewpoints overlooking the Mediterranean. Despite its rich history, it also has excellent sandy beaches — such as [El Miracle]({{< relref "/beaches/el-miracle" >}}), close to the centre, and the more spacious and calm [Arrabassada]({{< relref "/beaches/arrabassada" >}}).
